## Implemented data contract

[FACT mlzoomcamp-2026-data-release] The car dataset has 10,000 rows and 11
columns. Displacement, cylinders, horsepower, vehicle weight, and acceleration
are generated as dependent measurements in that order, with reference means of
about 2,269 cc, 6 cylinders, 254 hp, 4,287 lb, and 18.9 seconds. The report
records zero bound violations, negative physical-feature/MPG and positive
year/MPG relationships, and conditional missingness for older horsepower
records and European acceleration records.

[FACT mlzoomcamp-2026-data-release] The lead dataset has 5,000 rows and 9
columns. Acquisition, employment, income, course views, interactions, and lead
score form an explicit dependency chain. Conversion is sampled from a
temperature-controlled, target-rate-calibrated logistic probability. The
interaction-count/lead-score correlation is approximately 0.929 and the
conversion rate is approximately 0.577.
