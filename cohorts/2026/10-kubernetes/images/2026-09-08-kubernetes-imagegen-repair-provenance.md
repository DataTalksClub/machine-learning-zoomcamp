## Native crop evidence and reproducibility

The reproducibility script is
[`2026-09-09-kubernetes-remaining-provenance-crops.sh`](2026-09-09-kubernetes-remaining-provenance-crops.sh).
It applies only ImageMagick `-crop 475x360+23+0`, `+repage`, and JPEG quality
100 to the original JPG. It performs no resize, Lanczos pass, sharpening, or
other enhancement. The script includes the six earlier source-backed crops,
the two repaired screenshot-derived inputs, and the definitions source-evidence
crop.

The three repaired outputs used both the full original JPG and the matching
native crop as imagegen inputs. The accepted built-in imagegen executions were
`exec-2a0b1faa-ba35-401d-9ec9-452e281f7f47` for Services,

imagegen candidates were not used as inputs to any published output.

## Semantic source support

- Services is supported by `05-kubernetes-intro.md:56-72`: two services,
  Gateway Service and Model Service; Gateway Service distributes to Gateway
  pods; each Gateway pod uses Model Service; Model Service routes to a
  TensorFlow Serving pod and the response returns to the user.
- External/Internal/Ingress is supported by
  `05-kubernetes-intro.md:79-92`: `LoadBalancer` is external, `ClusterIP`
  is internal, and clients contact `Ingress` first.
- Definitions is supported by `05-kubernetes-intro.md:50-52` and `:79-92`.
  Exact labels are rendered natively in the SVG, including `LoadBalancer`,
  `ClusterIP`, and `same image and configuration`.
- The Explore More visual is a prompt-native exception, not screenshot
  crispification. Its namespace with model and gateway services, local versus
  managed-cloud split, and same deployment pattern are supported by
  `10-explore-more.md:4-14`. The managed-cloud examples are also consistent
  with the EKS lesson's explicit Amazon cloud deployment at
  `08-eks.md:6-10` and its load-balancer → gateway → internal model-service →
  TF-Serving flow at `08-eks.md:151-155`. No source JPG or native crop is
  fabricated for this new conceptual visual.
