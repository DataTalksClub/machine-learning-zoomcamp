## Machine Learning Zoomcamp 2026

The cohort starts on 14 September 2026.

* [Register here](https://courses.datatalks.club/register/ml-zoomcamp/)
* [Course management platform](https://courses.datatalks.club/ml-zoomcamp-2026/)
* [FAQ](https://datatalks.club/faq/machine-learning-zoomcamp.html)
* [Leaderboard](https://courses.datatalks.club/ml-zoomcamp-2026/leaderboard)

Homework assignments marked `[DRAFT]` are carried over from the 2025 cohort and will be
finalized before the module starts.

### Deadlines

All deadlines are on Mondays at 23:00 UTC. Submit everything on the [course platform](https://courses.datatalks.club/ml-zoomcamp-2026/).

| Module | Deadline |
|--------|----------|
| 1. Introduction to Machine Learning | 21 September 2026 |
| 2. Machine Learning for Regression | 28 September 2026 |
| 3. Machine Learning for Classification | 5 October 2026 |
| 4. Evaluation Metrics for Classification | 12 October 2026 |
| 5. Deploying Machine Learning Models | 19 October 2026 |
| 6. Decision Trees and Ensemble Learning | 26 October 2026 |
| 8. Neural Networks and Deep Learning | 23 November 2026 |
| 9. Serverless Deep Learning | 30 November 2026 |
| 10. Kubernetes and TensorFlow Serving | 7 December 2026 |

Projects have two deadlines: one for submitting your own project, and one for reviewing three projects from other students. Skipping the peer reviews fails the project.

| Project | Submission | Peer review |
|---------|------------|-------------|
| Midterm | 9 November 2026 | 16 November 2026 |
| Capstone 1 | 4 January 2027 | 11 January 2027 |
| Capstone 2 | 18 January 2027 | 25 January 2027 |

You need two passing projects for a certificate: midterm and one capstone, or both capstones.
